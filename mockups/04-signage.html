Signage
Airport wayfinding, applied literally. Flat sign panels, one tight grotesk at real scale, pictograms and direction arrows. Colour is functional the way it is in a terminal: green means go this way, red means restricted. No accent-on-black glow, no cards — the verdict is a sign you read from across the concourse.
